Garmin health/sleep recovery dashboard, 2026-11-07

Scope: optional private dashboard at /recovery, separate from activity sync.
Feeds: dailies, sleeps, hrv, stressDetails (PUSH JSON). No Training/Courses or
FIT-file integration is added in this release. No history pull/backfill.

Official references read: Garmin Health API 2.1 (2026-10-21), and the live
wellness-api OpenAPI schema at https://apis.garmin.com/tools/apiDocs/wellness-api.
https://developerportal.garmin.com/sites/default/files/Health_API_2.1.pdf

The receiver keeps only normalized recovery summaries; raw sensor maps,
coordinates and other health categories are discarded. Body Battery retains
the latest valid level and its observation time, rather than its raw series.
The existing source-IP and Garmin client-ID checks protect the webhook.

Migration: apps/d1-migration/migrations/0037_garmin_health_sync.sql.
Applied to production D1 successfully before deployment. Existing activity
records, account permissions and health consent were not changed by migration.

Health summaries enter an owner-specific durable inbox only when the connection
has HEALTH_EXPORT and separate health-sleep-v1 consent. Processing rechecks the
same consent generation. Stop deletes summaries and queued health receipts;
disconnect, permission withdrawal and account deletion also clear health data.
Normalized records are retained for 90 days; delivery receipts expire in 7 days
and their payloads are cleared after processing. Server routes require JWT auth
and set private/no-store. Provider tokens remain encrypted in existing storage.

Updated summary windows replace prior windows by owner/type/start time; shorter
late retries cannot replace a longer window. Daily totals and multiple devices
are not added together. The dashboard shows the longest sleep window per Garmin
calendar date. Awake time is separate from sleep duration. Tentative sleep is
labeled and excluded from sleep baselines, along with manual/off-wrist sleep.

Baselines: median over distinct prior Garmin dates within 14 days; at least 7
dates are required. The latest day is excluded. The chart window can be 7 or 28
days and does not limit the baseline lookback. Missing readings remain null and
chart gaps stay visible. Each metric shows its own date; older observations are
labeled. Garmin score qualifiers are preserved. There is no synthetic readiness
score, medical diagnosis or training clearance claim.

Privacy: /privacy#garmin-health; separate consent, export and stop/delete controls
on /recovery. Garmin health, sleep and derived trends do not enter AI, Strava,
messaging or coaching-email pipelines. Activity totals remain separate.

Verification: dedicated health tests cover normalization, missing sensors,
updates/retries, consent, permission revocation, owner isolation, deletion,
retention, calendar dates, baselines and authenticated routes. Original Garmin,
Strava and tenant-security checks pass. Frontend and D1 backend builds pass;
Worker strict TypeScript passes. The full repository retains pre-existing
TypeScript diagnostics, with none in this feature's files. Browser fixture
checks cover consent, populated/empty/outage states and 390px mobile layout.
Synthetic readings were used only locally, never inserted in production.
