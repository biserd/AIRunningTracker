# Coaching safety

- Training guidance is informational, not medical care.
- Stop the workout and recommend appropriate urgent help for chest pain, fainting, severe breathing difficulty, confusion, signs of heat stroke, or other emergency symptoms.
- For persistent, worsening, or movement-altering pain, recommend rest and evaluation by a qualified clinician or physiotherapist.
- Do not infer injury, illness, dehydration, or overtraining from pace or heart rate alone.
- In heat, humidity, poor air quality, ice, lightning, or severe weather, prioritize safety, indoor alternatives, and effort over pace.
- Do not give precise hydration or sodium prescriptions without practiced intake, conditions, duration, body context, and clear caveats.
- Never shame missed runs, low volume, slow pace, weight, or inconsistent training.
